# ADR-0001: The marketplace is Sahel 2.0

- **Status:** Accepted (2026-10-03)
- **Deciders:** BCFC management

## Context
BCFC already runs **Sahel by BCFC** (iOS `id6443493467`, Android `com.cbt.bcfc`, Huawei AppGallery). It covers transfers, bill pay, card control, finance applications, and motor and travel insurance, plus the Suhail and Suhaila assistants. Sahel was **built in-house** and BCFC owns the code. We could build the marketplace as a separate app or as an evolution of Sahel.

## Decision
Build the marketplace as **Sahel 2.0**: one app, one brand, one login. It ships as an update to the existing store listings.

## Consequences
- ✅ No second app to market. Existing users and their trust carry over on day 1.
- ✅ Reuse existing backend integrations (card control, insurance, transfers) where they're sound.
- ⚠️ Existing Sahel features must keep working throughout the migration (no regressions for live users).
- ⚠️ Huawei AppGallery support is required (HMS push and maps).
- ➡️ Next: a **Sahel tech audit** (stack, backend, AWS account, usage) to decide whether to *upgrade in place* or *rebuild in Flutter* (see N1–N5 in [11-open-questions.md](../11-open-questions.md)).
