# 08 — Key User Journeys

## J1. Onboarding (target < 3 minutes)

1. Download the app (or update Sahel) and choose Arabic or English.
2. **"Log in with eKey"** → returns verified name, CPR, nationality, date of birth, and address.
   - Fallback: scan the CPR card (NFC) + selfie liveness check.
3. Create a PIN and enable biometrics. The device is bound.
4. Employment: employer and salary. Choose: **connect your bank (Open Banking)** or **upload a salary certificate**.
5. Consent screen: CRB pull + Open Banking (plain language, time-limited).
6. **The pre-approval wallet appears:** "Car up to BHD 18,000 · Personal up to BHD 9,000 · Card limit BHD 1,500."
7. Home screen is personalized, and every price is now a monthly figure for *this* customer.

## J2. Buy a car with Islamic finance (target < 15 minutes of customer effort)

1. Browse cars: filter by "≤ BHD 200/month," "Islamic," "SUV."
2. Open a listing (e.g., NMC new car) → 360° view → toggle **Murabaha / conventional** → see both monthly figures and total costs.
3. Optional: **trade in** your current car → photos + plate → instant valuation range → applied to the down payment.
4. Book a test drive, or skip to **Reserve** (BHD 100 deposit via Apple Pay or BenefitPay).
5. Choose motor **Takaful** from the comparison (Tasheelat Insurance) → it's added to the bundle.
6. Review the offer → **step-up auth** → e-sign the Murabaha *promise to purchase*.
7. Back office: BCFC buys the car from the dealer and takes ownership (recorded) → sale contract to the customer → e-sign.
8. Registration and insurance issued → delivery is scheduled → the car appears in **My Garage** with the installment schedule and autopay.

## J3. Instant credit card

1. Tap "IMTIAZ World" → the eligibility check is already done from pre-approval.
2. Confirm the limit → e-sign → **virtual card issued**.
3. "Add to Apple Wallet / Google Pay" via push provisioning → use it immediately.
4. The physical card is couriered; activate it in the app.

## J4. Home with Ijara (target conditional approval in < 10 minutes, completion in < 30 days)

1. **"How much home can I afford?"** → conditional approval certificate (shareable PDF / QR code for brokers).
2. Browse TRESCO and partner properties → book a viewing → make an offer.
3. Order a **TRESCO valuation** in the app → pay the fee → report in 3–5 days.
4. Final offer: Ijara Muntahia Bittamleek vs. conventional mortgage, side by side.
5. Document checklist tracker → e-sign → SLRB registration milestone → disbursement to the seller.
6. Property appears in **My Home** with the schedule, value tracking, and home-insurance renewal.

## J5. Pay an installment (target < 20 seconds)

1. Push notification: "Your car installment of BHD 185 is due in 3 days."
2. Tap → Apple Pay / BenefitPay / saved card → done → receipt + rewards points.
3. Or turn on **Autopay** once and forget it.

## J6. Motor claim (target < 5 minutes to file)

1. My Garage → "I had an accident" → location auto-filled → photos of the damage + police report (if any).
2. AI damage estimate → choose a garage → claim filed with the insurer through Tasheelat Insurance.
3. Track status; a replacement car is offered from Tasheelat Car Leasing.

## J7. Dealer: instant financing on the showroom floor

1. The salesperson opens the **Dealer Portal** → scans the customer's QR code (with the customer's consent) → sees the pre-approved amount.
2. Selects the car → creates an offer → the customer gets a push notification → accepts in their own app.
3. The dealer sees "Approved – ready to deliver" plus a payout timeline.
